01Overview

Supply chain sustainability at The Corporate

The Corporate is committed to net-zero across its full value chain by 2045. Most of its carbon footprint is created in its supply chain, not inside its own plants. Supplier performance therefore decides whether the commitment is met.

The programme implements The Corporate's Global Environmental Policy. The Supplier Code of Conduct makes its requirements binding on suppliers.

  • 71% of The Corporate's carbon footprint sits in the value chain (Scope 3).
  • 500+ Tier 1 suppliers take part in the programme.
  • 2045 is the net-zero target year across the full value chain.
  • 50% emissions reduction by 2030, against the 2023 base year.

02Why we are contacting you

Why we are contacting you

You are a Tier 1 supplier to The Corporate. This is why that matters now.

We must report what we cannot yet see

Under the EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD), The Corporate reports value-chain performance. Supplier data is the evidence.

Suppliers are the largest lever

Reducing emissions in the supply chain is the single biggest action available to The Corporate on its path to net-zero.

Due diligence is a legal expectation

Risk-based supply chain due diligence follows the German Supply Chain Act (LkSG) and the EU Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD).

Ask once

Suppliers with a recognised third-party rating are not asked to repeat the work. Everyone else completes one structured assessment. It is the start of a working relationship, not a one-off audit. Strong performers earn preferential standing.

04Your action

What you can do

Do you have an EcoVadis Scorecard?

A valid scorecard is one issued within the last 12 months.

Route A: submit your EcoVadis scorecard

  1. Confirm your scorecard was issued within the last 12 months.
  2. Open EcoVadis and share your scorecard with The Corporate.
  3. You are finished. You are exempt from the detailed assessment and do not complete the questionnaire.

The Corporate validates EcoVadis submissions weekly.
