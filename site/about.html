Supplier Sustainability Portal

About this tool

What it is for

This page explains The Corporate's supply chain sustainability programme to Tier 1 suppliers and takes their submission for the 2027 cycle. Suppliers with an EcoVadis scorecard record a short entry and share the scorecard on EcoVadis. All other suppliers complete the questionnaire on this page, by form or by uploading the filled Excel workbook.

Who runs it

The Corporate, Group Procurement and EHS and Sustainability. Contact: inbox@thecorporate.com.

What it stores, and about whom

  • Your company name and registered country.
  • The contact person's name, title and email address.
  • The answers and evidence notes you submit, and your EcoVadis scorecard link if you give one.
  • The declaration: the authorised signatory's name, the date and the confirmation.
  • The Excel workbook, if you upload one, exactly as you uploaded it.

Nothing is saved until you confirm your submission. A new submission with the same company name and contact email replaces the earlier one.

Where it is kept and processed

The data is held on Supabase, in its EU region (Frankfurt). The page is served by Netlify. No other provider receives your data.

Data notice

The Corporate (inbox@thecorporate.com) stores this data to run its 2027 supplier sustainability programme and to meet its sustainability reporting obligations, on the basis of contract or legitimate interest. It is kept until you ask for it to be deleted. It is held on Supabase (EU region) and served by Netlify. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by contacting inbox@thecorporate.com, and you can complain to your data protection authority.

How long it is kept, and your rights

Your data is kept until you ask for it to be deleted. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by contacting inbox@thecorporate.com, and you can complain to your data protection authority. A deletion removes every submission from your company and contact email, including the uploaded files.

Logins, cookies and tracking

There is no login and no supplier account. This page uses no cookies and no tracking.
